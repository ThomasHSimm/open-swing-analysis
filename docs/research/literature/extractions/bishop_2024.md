# Bishop et al. 2024 — TrackMan 4 Reliability and Inter-relationships of Launch Monitor Metrics

**Citation**: Bishop, C.; Wells, J.; Ehlert, A.; Turner, A.; Coughlan, D.; Sachs, N.; Murray, A. Trackman 4: Within and between-session reliability and inter-relationships of launch monitor metrics during indoor testing in high-level golfers. *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 2024.

**DOI**: 10.1080/02640414.2024.2314864

**Study type**: Repeated measures test-retest reliability study. Two sessions, maximum 7 days apart.

**Population**: n=13 male golfers (10 amateur, 3 professional). Age 33.0±7.8 years, height 176.4±7.7cm, body mass 72.8±12.7kg, handicap 7.6±7.5. All played minimum twice/week, minimum 7 years experience. Indoor golf academy setting.

**Methods**: 10 driver shots per session, self-selected warm-up (10–15 min). TrackMan 4 positioned 2.5m behind ball in normalised mode, aligned to predetermined target. Mean of 10 trials used for analysis. Sessions separated by maximum 7 days.

**Outcome measures**: 8 TrackMan metrics — clubhead speed (CHS, mph), ball speed (mph), carry distance (yards), smash factor (ball speed/CHS), spin rate (°/s), launch angle (°), attack angle (°), dynamic loft (°).

**Key findings**:

*Within-session reliability*:
- CHS: ICC=0.99 both sessions, SEM=1.64–1.67 mph — **excellent**
- Ball speed: ICC=0.97–0.99, SEM=2.46–4.42 mph — **excellent**
- Carry distance: ICC=0.91–0.94, SEM=10.65–14.21 yards — **excellent**
- Attack angle: ICC=0.82 both sessions — **good**
- Smash factor: ICC=0.52–0.70 — **moderate**
- Launch angle: ICC=0.43–0.67 — **moderate to poor**
- Dynamic loft: ICC=0.47–0.63 — **moderate to poor**
- Spin rate: ICC=0.02–0.60 — **poor to moderate** (ICC dropped to 0.02 in session 2)

*Between-session reliability*:
- CHS: ICC=0.99, SEM=1.66 mph — **excellent**, no systematic bias
- Ball speed: ICC=0.98, SEM=3.54 mph — **excellent**, no systematic bias
- Carry distance: ICC=0.97, SEM=7.80 yards — **excellent**, no systematic bias
- Significant systematic bias between sessions for: smash factor (g=0.47), spin rate (g=−0.41), dynamic loft (g=−0.21)

*Inter-relationships*:
- CHS and ball speed: r=0.98–0.99 (nearly perfect, both sessions)
- CHS and carry distance: r=0.94–0.95 (nearly perfect)
- Ball speed and carry distance: r=0.97–0.98 (nearly perfect)
- Launch angle and dynamic loft: r=0.98–0.99 (nearly perfect, but both unreliable)

**Practical implication for trial design**: Using 2×SEM as the minimal detectable change threshold — for CHS, any change >3.32 mph (2×1.66) can be considered greater than measurement error with ~95% confidence. For ball speed, threshold is >7.08 mph. For carry distance, >15.60 yards.

**Statistical approach**: Two-way random effects ICC with 95% CI. SEM = SD×√(1−ICC). Systematic bias via paired t-tests (p<0.05). Hedges g effect sizes. Pearson's r for inter-relationships. ICC thresholds: <0.5 poor, 0.5–0.74 moderate, 0.75–0.9 good, >0.9 excellent (Koo & Li 2016).

## Relevance to Trial Design

**Directly answers the secondary outcome reliability question.** If your trial uses TrackMan (or GCQuad, which is comparable technology), this paper provides the reliability benchmarks for CHS, ball speed, and carry distance. All three are excellent and suitable as secondary outcomes in a pre-post intervention study.

**Minimum detectable change values for your trial:**
- CHS: >3.32 mph between-session change needed to exceed measurement error (95% CI)
- Ball speed: >7.08 mph
- Carry distance: >15.60 yards

Compare these against expected effect sizes from the intervention literature: Li et al. found +5.73% CHS (~2.3 mph on a 40 m/s baseline, ~5.2 mph) and +11.3% ball speed. Belotti found ~2.7 m/s (~6 mph) club speed increase. These are in the range of or above the SEM thresholds, suggesting TrackMan is sensitive enough to detect the changes your intervention might produce — but only just for club speed.

**Spin rate and dynamic loft are not usable** as outcome measures in a multi-session study. Poor reliability, systematic session bias. Do not include these.

**Smash factor has caution flags** — significant between-session bias, ratio metric problems. Use only alongside component parts (CHS + ball speed separately).

**Indoor vs outdoor caveat**: Authors note TrackMan is better suited outdoors (radar tracks full ball flight). Indoor use, as in this study, is adequate for CHS and ball speed but carry distance is simulated. If your trial measures on a driving range outdoors, reliability may be marginally better for distance; if indoor, these figures apply directly.

## Limitations

**Noted by authors**: Small n=13; driver only (no iron data); indoor only; mixed handicap sample may reduce reliability estimates for within-group homogeneity.

**Additional**: No data for 5-iron or 6-iron (the club used by Faux 2019 and Belotti 2024). Reliability of irons likely similar for CHS and ball speed but untested here. Sample handicap range (0–20 implied) is wide — reliability in a homogeneous recreational sample may differ slightly.

---

## Quality Assessment

**High** for its purpose. Well-designed test-retest study, appropriate sample size calculation, correct statistical approach (ICC + SEM + systematic bias), open access. Same author group as Watson 2026. Limited scope (driver only, indoor only) but directly useful.

## Flags for Trial Design

1. **Use CHS and ball speed as your secondary performance outcomes, not carry distance.** Carry distance SEM (7.80–14.21 yards) is large relative to expected intervention effects and is simulated indoors. CHS and ball speed have tighter SEMs and are directly measured.

2. **Your minimal detectable change threshold for CHS is ~3.3 mph.** The Li et al. effect (~5.2 mph) just clears this; Belotti's acute effect (~6 mph) clears it more comfortably. A 4-week training study in recreational golfers should be detectable if the effect is real.

3. **This paper covers TrackMan reliability only — not force plate reliability.** The GRF/CoP reliability gap identified by Watson et al. remains unaddressed by this paper. You still need either a force plate reliability citation specific to golf, or to build a reliability session into your design.

4. **Protocol note**: 10 shots per session with self-selected warm-up, mean of all 10 used. This is the validated protocol for TrackMan data collection. Match it in your trial.
