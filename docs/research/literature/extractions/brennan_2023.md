# Brennan et al. 2023 — FlightScope Mevo+ validity & reliability vs TrackMan 4

**Register tier:** Evidence — *device selection*. The one paper of the three to keep as a primary evidence entry, IF a Mevo+ is used for ball-flight outcomes. Within-session only.

**Citation**: Brennan A, Murray A, Coughlan D, Mountjoy M, Wells J, Ehlert A, Xu J, Broadie M, Turner A, Bishop C. Validity and reliability of the FlightScope Mevo+ launch monitor for assessing golf performance. 2023. (peer-review copy; affiliations London Sport Institute / The R&A / DP World Tour et al.)

**DOI**: not present in PDF (ethics committee redacted for review) — to be sourced.

**Study type**: Concurrent validity (vs TrackMan 4) + within-session reliability. Cross-sectional, single session.

**Population**: n=29 youth golfers (25 M, 4 F), age 16.85 ± 0.55 y, mean handicap 6.7 ± 4.7, ≥3 y experience. A priori power: 21 needed (power 0.8, α 0.05, d 0.5).

**Intervention**: None.

**Comparator**: TrackMan 4 as criterion ("benchmark standard"). Devices recorded concurrently.

**Duration**: Single session; 10 shots each club (6-iron first, then driver), 60 s between shots, 3 min between clubs. Maximal-effort instruction.

**Outcome measures**: 8 metrics — CHS, ball speed, carry, smash factor, spin rate, launch angle, attack angle, spin axis.

**Statistical approach**: Pearson r (validity), Bland–Altman (agreement), paired t-tests + Cohen's d (device differences), ICC + CV + SEM (reliability). Koo & Li ICC bands; CV good <5 / mod 5–10 / poor >10. Device cost context: Mevo+ ~£2k vs TrackMan 4 ~£20k+.

**Key findings — which Mevo+ metrics are usable**:

| Metric | Mevo+ vs TrackMan | Mevo+ verdict |
|---|---|---|
| CHS | r ≥ 0.92–0.96; bias 1.7–2.0 mph; CV <2% | Trustworthy |
| Ball speed | r ≥ 0.92; bias ~1 mph; CV <5% | Trustworthy |
| Carry distance | r ~0.88; CV ~7% | Usable, noisier |
| Smash factor | bias ~−0.01; near-perfect agreement | Usable (ratio caveat) |
| Spin rate | r 0.33–0.37; bias 863–1238 rpm; Mevo+ ICC −0.22 to 0.12; sig diff (d 0.90–1.27) | Do not use on Mevo+ |
| Launch angle | sig diff on driver (d 0.54) | Caution (OK-ish 6-iron) |
| Attack angle | sig diff on driver (d −0.51) | Caution |
| Spin axis | low r, high noise | Caution |

**Relevance to trial design**:
- *Device choice*: if budget forces a Mevo+ and ball flight is a secondary outcome, CHS / ball speed / carry / smash factor are defensible. Spin rate on Mevo+ is effectively unusable.
- *Confounder*: maximal-distance instruction was used; authors note shot intent changes strategy-metric variability. If pre/post instructions differ (or differ from this protocol), reliability will not carry over. Standardise and report.
- *Population*: youth, handicap 6.7. Authors say reliability may improve with higher-standard players — read-across to a different pilot population is uncertain.
- *Effect sizes / duration / balance / CoP*: N/A.

**Limitations noted**: maximal-distance instruction may inflate strategy-metric variability; usability may depend on skill level; sample is youth handicap 6.7 — higher-standard players untested.

**Quality assessment**: Medium for its own purpose; **Low–Medium for this trial** — device-selection evidence only, within-session only.

**Direct quotes worth keeping**: none essential; paraphrase.

**Flags / contradictions**:
1. **Within-session only.** Establishes device agreement + same-session consistency, NOT the between-session (test–retest) error a pre/post design needs. The between-session Mevo+ source is cited (Villarrasa-Sapiña et al., Sensors 2022, ref 18) but not reproduced here. → TODO if Mevo+ is the chosen device.
2. CV-vs-ICC artefact handled correctly here (unlike Shaw): authors state CV inflates for near-zero metrics and that ICC/SEM are the better read.
3. **Implausible value, Table 2:** driver attack angle TrackMan 10.75 ± 36.03°, CV 230%. SD of 36° is not physically real for driver attack angle (~−5° to +5° range); the abstract's mean bias (−1.86°) is inconsistent with table means (Mevo+ −2.53 vs TrackMan +10.75) and with d = −0.51. That row is contaminated — do not cite the driver attack-angle numbers.
4. **Broken CI, Table 3:** 6-iron launch angle Mevo+ CV "9.61 (2.47, 7.14)" — point estimate outside its own CI. Transcription error.
5. Minor abstract/results mismatch on TrackMan ICC lower bound (0.60 vs 0.63).
